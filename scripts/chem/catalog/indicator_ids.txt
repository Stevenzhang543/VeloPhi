# The mixed colour between two forms follows from the equilibrium (the amounts of both forms) and Beer's law; green for
# bromothymol blue at pH 7, orange for methyl orange at pH 3.5 to 4 and purple for litmus near pH 6.5 need no species.
#
# The universal indicator is a reagent, not a species: Yamada's recipe (header of supplement_indicators.dat) holds
# thymol blue, methyl red, bromothymol blue and phenolphthalein; its colour is the sum of their absorbances.
#
# Two colours of the experiments have no species yet (asked of the network's owner, not defined here):
#   the blue of starch with iodine (starch + triiodide), and the violet of phenol with iron(III).
